# Service Records – Lubricants

### Purpose
Service Records track lubricant servicing performed on vehicles and award points accordingly.

### Create a service record
1. Go to **Dealer → Service Records – Lubricants**.
2. Click **New Service Records – Lubricants**.
3. Select or create a vehicle.
4. Enter service details:
   - Service date
   - Dealer
   - Mileage
5. Upload invoice image if available.
6. Click **Next** to review.
7. Submit the service record.

### Update service status
- Draft records can be edited before submission.
- Submitted records are locked from editing.
