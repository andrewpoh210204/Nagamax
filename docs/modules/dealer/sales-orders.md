# Sales Orders

### Purpose
Sales Orders are used to record and submit product sales made by dealers. These orders generate sales records and dealer points.

### Create a sales order
1. Go to **Dealer → Sales Orders**.
2. Click **New Sales Order**.
3. Add order items:
   - Select an item.
   - List price is automatically populated based on dealer-specific pricing.
   - Enter quantity.
4. Review total amount and total dealer points.
5. Click **Create** to save the order.

### Update or submit a sales order
- Draft orders can be edited or cancelled.
- Submit the order once all items are confirmed.
- Submitted orders may later be synced or cancelled depending on status.
