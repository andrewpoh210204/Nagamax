# Warranty Records – Batteries

### Purpose
Warranty Records track battery warranties registered for vehicles and customers.

### Create a warranty record
1. Go to **Dealer → Warranty Records – Batteries**.
2. Click **New Warranty Records – Battery**.
3. Select the dealer.
4. Enter purchase date.
5. Select the battery item.
6. Fill in vehicle details:
   - Plate number
   - Car model
   - Year made
7. Enter customer details:
   - Name
   - Phone number
   - Email (optional)
8. Upload supporting documents if available:
   - Vehicle photo with registration number
   - Odometer reading
   - Receipt image
9. Click **Create**.

### View warranty records
- Use search and filters to locate records.
- View warranty status and validity period.
